## Reporting as a Service (Current Initiative)

The team is currently working on separating the Risk Management reporting module into an independent Reporting-as-a-Service architecture.

Objectives include:

- Isolating reporting workloads from core business services.
- Improving scalability.
- Independent deployment and maintenance.
- Better resource utilization.
- Easier future expansion of reporting capabilities.

Ravi contributes to the frontend and backend development involved in this architectural evolution.
